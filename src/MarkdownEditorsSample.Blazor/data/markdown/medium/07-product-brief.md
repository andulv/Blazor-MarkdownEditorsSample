# Product Brief (Mock)

The objective is to provide a practical markdown editing surface in a Blazor app.

## Constraints

- Keep interop manageable.
- Preserve markdown fidelity where possible.
- Support simple authoring for non-technical users.

## Non-goals

- Not building a full CMS.
- Not supporting every markdown flavor extension.

## Acceptance

A tester can open multiple sample files and compare the editing experience without setup friction.
